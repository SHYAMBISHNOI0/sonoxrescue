## Limitations

Shared synthetic generator families can favor explicit rules. These results establish repeatability and characterize safety/coverage tradeoffs, not hospital performance. No learned model is trained. Separate seeds provide independent draws but do not remove template-family overlap. Root-cause accuracy uses at-least-one-cause overlap, which is weaker than exact reconstruction. Review workload is flagged-or-abstained fraction. Detection latency in the operational demo is a configured offset; benchmark latency is actual processing time.
